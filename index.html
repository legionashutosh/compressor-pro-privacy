Privacy Policy for Compressor Pro

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Compressor Pro ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") is a file compression application designed to help users compress photos, videos, and PDF files on Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Compressor Pro.

1. File Processing

Compressor Pro is designed to process selected photos, videos, and PDF files locally on your device. Files selected for compression are not intentionally uploaded to our own servers for the compression process.

You control which files you select and process through the application. Compressed files are saved according to the options and permissions available on your device.

2. Personal Information

Compressor Pro does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or password in order to use the core compression features.

3. Advertising

Compressor Pro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from your device for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Advertising-related information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, IP address, app interactions, and information about advertisements delivered or interacted with, depending on your device, settings, region, and applicable consent choices.

For more information about how Google handles information in connection with advertising, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Compressor Pro uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide users with privacy and consent choices relating to advertising and related data processing.

Your available choices may depend on your location, applicable laws, device settings, and Google's advertising technologies.

5. Permissions

The application may request access to files or media on your device when necessary to allow you to select files for compression or save compressed results.

Permissions are used for the functionality they are requested for. You can manage applicable permissions through your Android device settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Compressor Pro may use third-party services and software libraries required for advertising, consent management, analytics, security, or application functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to design Compressor Pro so that files selected for compression can be processed locally on the device. However, no electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Compressor Pro is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed toward children.

9. Data Retention

Compressor Pro does not maintain a personal account database for users. Files created or selected by the application may remain on your device according to your device's storage behavior and the actions you take within the application.

Advertising and consent-related information may be retained or processed by third-party service providers according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Your Choices

You can choose which files to process, manage application permissions through Android settings, and make available advertising consent choices when presented by the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Compressor Pro, you can contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
